## Why this is a different kind of purchasing decision

A cruise traveler may have only a few hours and must decide whether buying an independently operated tour is acceptable compared with a cruise-line-protected excursion or staying close to the ship. A generic marketplace filters by destination/activity and price but may not normalize *actual terminal*, **ship vs local time**, earliest ashore, check-in, travel time and pre-all-aboard reserve. This decision engine asks the buyer to resolve those information gaps and only then provides a **provisional** window calculation.

It intentionally does **not** pick a “best excursion” or claim two unlike activities offer comparable value. Group prices are user-entered, USD-only, price-per-traveler × party plus explicitly confirmed total fees. Unknown/additional fees never mean zero. “Window fits” means arithmetic under given assumptions, not actual booking availability, reliable transport, travel safety or a guarantee of return.

## Honest affiliate economics

Viator's **public** claim of 8% on completed bookings illustrates an *unearned* opportunity: a `$150` completed attributable sale would notionally yield `$12` gross commission **only if** a contractual relationship and valid tracking existed and terms held. This project has **zero** approved Viator account, tracked sales, observed conversion, SEO traffic, referral acceptance or revenue. Further cancellations, discounts, attribution restrictions and payout timing must be confirmed in an actual agreement. Do not display affiliate code, prices from operators, commissions, clickable tour affiliate listings or purchase buttons without that evidence.

No planned partner is promised, no invitation is sent, and no tour inventory is imported. Any official/source external link is factual context, not an offer. This is a protected decision-planner experiment, not a publicly bookable excursion search.

## Release and privacy gates

- Hosted preview must have Vercel **Authentication/SSO for all deployments** and remain not indexed. The user must explicitly authorize opening the site.
- Unit and browser tests must confirm unknown timing, pier, ferry, missed-port policy, extra fees and invalid numerical data fail closed, and inputs aren't sent to external analytics.
- Treat the independent operator's own “back on time” marketing as not equivalent to a cruise-line's own official guarantee.
- No operational promise of traffic, conversions, rate, income, or partner acceptance.
