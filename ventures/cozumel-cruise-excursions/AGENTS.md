# Agent operating rules — Shore Window

- Independent Cozumel cruise-excursion pilot only. Do not import or alter the parent Home Improvement app or build a generalized global cruise-engine without verified partner data.
- Use official cruise-line FAQs for all-aboard and ship-tour protection; their rules do not apply to independently purchased tours.
- The three Cozumel terminals are not interchangeable. Never infer berth from ship brand. Operator meeting-point instructions are not official authorization.
- Every time in a deterministic calculation is in *verified ship time*. The ship's **all-aboard**, not generic departure, is the deadline. Never auto-convert Cozumel local time from a guessed offset.
- Treat schedule fit as a tentative arithmetic screen, never safety/ship-return guarantee, real-time inventory, confirmed tour availability or insurance. All mainland ferries fail closed for manual review.
- No fabricated product availability, agent partner acceptance, tour prices, operator pickups, commission eligibility or return guarantees. Viator 8% is a public program example, NOT our payout.
- Inputs must remain browser-local. Do not add analytics with individual itinerary/party/price, contact collection, persistent state or third-party APIs without new owner consent and privacy review.
- Remain SSO protected and noindex; no affiliate links, booking or public launch. For changes, add node unit tests and desktop/mobile Playwright; the latter must actually run, not merely be listed.
- Keep this as a standalone `ventures/cozumel-cruise-excursions/` Vite app that can later be extracted into its own repo. Never merge automatically.
