# Independent official-source review — November 3, 2026

## Official pages checked

1. [Portland residential mechanical permits](https://www.portland.gov/ppd/residential-permitting/residential-mechanical-permits): heat pumps, mini-splits and AC condensers among listed projects requiring mechanical permits; most mechanical equipment work also needs an electrical permit. Final mechanical inspection required. Check outdoor unit setbacks and any historic-resource constraints.
2. [Portland residential permit decision page](https://www.portland.gov/ppd/residential-permitting/do-you-need-permit/residential-permits): additions/alterations to hard-wired systems require electrical permits; residential exterior heat pumps also listed for mechanical permitting.
3. [Portland residential electrical permits](https://www.portland.gov/ppd/residential-permitting/residential-electrical-permits): wiring/panel modification permitting and final electrical inspection. Owner-occupant versus rental contractor restrictions.
4. [Oregon Heat Pump Purchase Program (HP3)](https://www.oregon.gov/energy/incentives/Pages/HP3.aspx): Oregon owner-occupied/rental/new residential construction potential eligibility; excludes commercial and vacation/transient occupancy; general HSPF2 >= 8.1, SEER2 >=16; one incentive per residence and stacking restrictions; approval requires ODOE-approved installer; final passed mechanical inspection.
5. HP3 funding **table last updated October 1, 2026**: owner-occupied funds $0 and 0 new incentives; rental and new-construction buckets had approximate availability. These are snapshots, not real-time quantities.

## Corrections

- Use an explicit funding snapshot date, distinct from date this code was reviewed.
- Never claim rental/new-construction funds are available today; never extrapolate Portland permits outside Portland.
- Explain that approved contractors provide qualifying HP3 incentives as an up-front discount, with ODOE reimbursing after eligible application. Incentive reservations are OPTIONAL, not generally mandatory preapproval; an approved reservation is not required prior to install under currently published rules.
- Mechanical permit final inspection is necessary to close the permit; program applications require passing final mechanical inspection except allowed minor-label cases.
- The numeric efficiency check is not an equipment approval and does not cover special PTHP policies.

## Launch blockers

- Confirm jurisdiction at the actual address; 'in Portland' may not identify all relevant site-specific permitting requirements.
- Current HP3 funding must be retrieved and marked fresh, or the site must remain a historical guide (not actionable eligibility calculator).
- Validate specific equipment and contractor quotes with official approved contractor list and ODOE policy.
- Independent reviewer should inspect the decision logic and a handful of actual project cases.
- No contractor referral arrangements, verified user conversions, or other real-world demand evidence.
